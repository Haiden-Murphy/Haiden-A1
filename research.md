## Student Portfolio Goal Reasearch
My goals are fairly simple, I mention them above as if I have already achived them, but I have not. I imagine myself five years from now, talking with an old friend. Based on these goals, I will do a research analysis using two articles accessible through Laurier's library that are related to student portfolio goals.

These articles both touch on how student planning and experiences contribute to and influence goal-making. I think my goals, at least when it comes to my career, are fairly planned out and not just thrown out there. For example, I interned at IBM for two summers. That, coupled with the completion of the UX program here at Laurier, increases my chances of working there sometime in the future. The second article on goal setting and student achievement focuses on how reflection helps create strong goals. I have reflected on this goal quite a bit, but on others not so much. For example, my goal is to travel to Italy with my family in the next five years. I haven't reflected on this much; maybe its not in the cards, or a vacation may take us somewhere else.
